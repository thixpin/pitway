# Contract

## Objective

Land the two structural items the v1.3.0 architecture review left open
(A4, A1), both behavior-preserving. A4 removes a test-time tax that has
turned every documentation-only task since M037 into a hash-regeneration
task, by retiring the M023 migration manifest on exactly the terms its
own comment set out; the durable content invariants it once backed are
already enforced by later, non-historical suites. A1 gives
`src/state/journal.ts` the same responsibility split M039 gave
`core/tasks/update.ts` -- schemas apart from I/O -- as a verbatim move
behind a re-export, so no importer changes.

## Scope / tasks

- T001 (A4) Retire the manifest block; snapshot the resolved asset set +
  content before and after and prove them identical.
- T002 (A1) Move the journal schemas/types to `journal-schemas.ts` with a
  re-export from `journal.ts`; extend the layering test.
- T003 Full gate (declares `verification.timeout_ms`, per M045/W1).

T001 and T002 are independent; T003 depends on both.

## Dependencies

- M045 merged (`14df48a`): T003 uses the task-level `timeout_ms`; scope
  entries are file-level by rule.
- M023's contract's own manifest lifecycle statement is the authority for
  A4.

## Non-Goals

- Any change to shipped asset bytes, the two-tier resolver, install
  destinations, drift detection, or protocol/command doc text.
- Retiring the resolution-equivalence, body-parity, partition-consistency,
  re-wrap, or stray-override suites -- they stay.
- Any journal schema, record kind, or validation change; any
  reconcilePending change; updating importers (the re-export makes that
  unnecessary).
- Usage-schema work (next milestone, re-scoped against
  docs/evidence/M042/synthesis.md section 9).
- Version bump, CHANGELOG, or release work.
